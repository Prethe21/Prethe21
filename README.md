## 🧠 About

Software engineer and AI/ML builder with a product-first mindset. I don't just train models, I ship them: APIs, interfaces, and the reliability layer that makes people trust the output.

My engineering focus splits three ways:

- **Applied AI & LLM systems:** Built and shipped a GPT-4 assistant into daily production use, engineering it to flag uncertainty instead of guessing, which let a team handle 10× more material per sprint.
- **Signal processing & sensor fusion:** Extracting reliable estimates from noisy, incomplete, and adversarial measurement data: irregular sampling, random dropout, full sensor blackout.
- **Agentic AI & language-model tooling:** From production prompt design to hackathon tools that read unstructured text and turn it into structured, human-approved records, with a growing focus on multi-step, tool-using AI workflows.

I treat documentation as a deliverable, not an afterthought, and I'm equally comfortable inside a PyTorch training loop or explaining a model's failure modes to a stakeholder who isn't a machine learning specialist.
